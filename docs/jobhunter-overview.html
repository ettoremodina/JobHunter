Chapter 01 · Overview

From 23,049 job ads to a short list of companies

JobHunter is a personal archive of companies and job ads. It collects broadly from several job boards, groups everything by company, and runs each ad past a series of judges, from the cheapest to the most careful. What remains is a short list, and the final say stays with the user.

JobHunter è un archivio personale di aziende e annunci di lavoro. Raccoglie in modo largo da più bacheche, raggruppa tutto per azienda e fa passare ogni annuncio davanti a una serie di giudici, dal più economico al più attento. Alla fine resta una lista corta, e la decisione finale resta all'utente.

Two separate questions: company and role

The tool answers two questions that don't coincide: which companies are interesting, whether or not they are hiring now, and which job ads fit the profile. A company can be interesting with no open positions; a role can fit in a company that isn't. So JobHunter judges company and role on two separate axes and combines them only at the end, into a Tier.

Il tool risponde a due domande che non coincidono: quali aziende sono interessanti, che stiano assumendo o no, e quali annunci sono compatibili con il profilo. Un'azienda può interessare senza posizioni aperte; un ruolo può essere adatto in un'azienda che non interessa. Per questo JobHunter giudica azienda e ruolo su due assi separati e li incrocia solo alla fine, in un Tier.

System overview

Design principles

Two independent axes

Company and role are judged separately. Neither can cancel the other.

Deciding and writing are separate

Jev answers with probabilities and writes no text. Qwen writes summaries and decides nothing.

Missing evidence is not a rejection

An ad without text waits. A company without data goes to a work queue.

Nothing computed is stored

The Tier is recomputed on every read, so a company moves up as soon as it posts the right role.

Spend only where needed

Free, certain checks first; paid ones after. Summaries are written only after the Tier.

Every verdict is signed

Each record keeps which judge decided what, and with which evidence.

Automatic re-evaluation

Each filter verdict is saved with two fingerprints: the ad text and the active rules. If either changes, the verdict is recomputed automatically, only for the ads affected.

Fetching job descriptions

Many sources give only a title and a link. This step opens the ad page and reads the text from the formats job sites publish for search engines. Each attempt is logged with its result and a date to retry. It never excludes anything: it only decides how much evidence the next judges will have.

Molte fonti danno solo titolo e link. Questo passaggio apre la pagina dell'annuncio e legge il testo dai formati che i siti pubblicano per i motori di ricerca. Ogni tentativo viene registrato con l'esito e una data per riprovare. Non esclude mai nulla: decide solo quanta evidenza avranno i giudici successivi.

Fetching follows company coverage, not the filters. At least one ad is fetched for every company with no other evidence, even if all its roles were discarded; otherwise the company would stay invisible.

Chapter 04 · User profile

User profile

The profile says who is looking for work and what they want. It isn't handed whole to one model: it's translated into a different form for each judge. Whatever can be written as a rule goes to the cheap judges; only what needs real understanding reaches a model.

Where a new preference goes

Changing what a model reads invalidates the judgements already paid for. So a preference goes to the cheap rules whenever it can be stated literally, and to the model only when it truly needs understanding.

The result: the more the tool is used, the more the rules filter, and the less work reaches the model. A choice about one company never becomes a rule for future ones.

Cambiare ciò che un modello legge invalida i giudizi già pagati. Per questo una preferenza va alle regole economiche quando si può scrivere in modo letterale, e al modello solo quando serve davvero capire il testo.

Il risultato: più il tool viene usato, più le regole filtrano e meno lavoro arriva al modello. Una scelta su una singola azienda non diventa mai una regola per quelle future.

Each copy of JobHunter has one profile. Setting it up for someone else means writing their own profile, not inheriting this one.

Chapter 05 · Role evaluation

Role evaluation

Two automatic judges look at each role. The rule filter can only discard what is clearly out of scope. Jev reads the duties and answers precise questions with probabilities. When neither is sure, the role is marked “unsure” and goes to the user.

Step 1: rule filter

The filter reads only the ad: the title, matched against lists of patterns, and the description, where it looks for required years of experience, people management and required languages. It has one possible outcome: discard. Everything else goes on to Jev.

Il filtro legge solo l'annuncio: il titolo, confrontato con elenchi di pattern, e la descrizione, dove cerca anni di esperienza obbligatori, gestione di persone e lingue richieste. Ha un solo esito possibile: scarta. Tutto il resto passa a Jev.

Experience requirements

Step 2: Jev

Jev is a “System One” model by TypeSafe. It receives a text and a list of questions, and for each one returns a probability (yes/no) or a choice among fixed options. It writes no text, so a missing answer or an invented quote is impossible. It sees every ad the filter didn't discard, as long as there is text to read.

Jev è un modello «System One» di TypeSafe. Riceve un testo e un elenco di domande, e per ciascuna restituisce una probabilità (sì/no) o una scelta fra opzioni fisse. Non scrive testo, quindi una risposta mancante o una citazione inventata sono impossibili. Vede ogni annuncio non scartato dal filtro, purché ci sia un testo da leggere.

Duties describedyes / no

Does the text say what the person will do, or only present the company?

Compatible dutiesyes / no

Is the work mainly quantitative analysis, modelling, or software on data and models?

Excluded familychoice

Does the main work belong to one of the excluded job families, or to none?

Student postyes / no

Is it an internship, a thesis, or a post for enrolled students?

Too senioryes / no

Does it ask for seniority, management or over two required years, in any language?

Evidencechoice

Which sentence of the ad best describes the work? The options are its real sentences.

How Jev's answers become a verdict

Jev doesn't give the verdict itself. It answers each question with a probability from 0 (certainly no) to 1 (certainly yes). A fixed checklist then turns those answers into keep, unsure or discard. The checklist is read from top to bottom, and the first line that applies decides. Pick an example or move the sliders.

Jev non dà il verdetto. Risponde a ogni domanda con una probabilità da 0 (sicuramente no) a 1 (sicuramente sì). Poi una checklist fissa trasforma le risposte in tieni, non so o scarta. La checklist si legge dall'alto in basso, e decide la prima riga che vale. Scegli un esempio o muovi i cursori.

Tiers

The Tier combines the company verdict with the verdict on its roles. It is never stored: it's recomputed every time, so a company moves up as soon as it posts the right role.

Three kinds of chat session

unsure cases

Review unsure cases

On roles Jev left “unsure”. The agent groups cases, asks questions and proposes rules, showing first how many ads they would move.

rules

Apply the rules

On unsure cases and compatible Tier A and B roles. The agent decides role by role, always citing a confirmed rule.

Explore the selection

On Tier A and B companies, filtered by country, city or category. Helps compare and choose; doesn't change the pipeline.

Chapter 09 · Traceability

Traceability

For every company and job ad, the archive records who decided what, with which evidence and on which version of the text. What can be recomputed isn't stored; what costs money or was written by the user is never lost.

Stored vs computed

stored, never deleted
  • original source snapshots
  • job ads, companies and where they were seen
  • every fetch attempt, with its result
  • Jev answers and Qwen summaries
  • user decisions and confirmed rules
computed on every read
  • filter result (self-updating cache)
  • company verdict
  • role verdict
  • chain of judges
  • Tier

Cache and re-evaluation

Every Jev answer is saved with a fingerprint of what produced it: text, questions, thresholds, sectors and model version. When something changes, only what that change affects goes back in the queue.
