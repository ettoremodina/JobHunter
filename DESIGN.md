# Dashboard design

Mode: Operate. The user's explicit priority is legibility and navigation.

Use a tokenised palette that follows the reader's light or dark preference: layered surfaces on a tinted page, blue actions, native forms and a visible keyboard focus ring. A paginated company table accompanies a readable detail panel. Charts are allowed only when they carry information the text cannot: the pipeline funnel draws complementary shares and the handover between stages. No external fonts, no maps, no motion beyond short state transitions. Colour never carries meaning alone: every share is also named and counted. On narrow screens the detail follows the table. Long descriptions are expandable. Status includes a textual label. Errors remain visible; failed saves do not remove data from the screen.
