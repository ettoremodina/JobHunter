# Dashboard design

Mode: Operate. The user's explicit priority is legibility and navigation.

Use a neutral light background, dark system text and blue actions, native forms and visible keyboard focus. A paginated company table accompanies a readable detail panel. No decorative charts, external fonts or maps. On narrow screens the detail follows the table. Long descriptions are expandable. Status includes a textual label. Errors remain visible; failed saves do not remove data from the screen.
