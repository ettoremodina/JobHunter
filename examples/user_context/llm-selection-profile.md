# Selection profile

Example file: a fictional candidate. Replace it with your own profile, derived from `portfolio-evidence.md`. The summary cards and the review agent read this file; the rule filter and Jev use their own configuration files.

## Who they are

MSc in Physics, about one year of full-time experience as a data analyst at an energy utility. Likes turning messy measurement data into forecasts and simple, explainable models.

## What they look for

Junior roles where the main work is data analysis, statistics, forecasting, machine learning or data engineering: data scientist, machine learning engineer, data analyst with modelling, analytics engineer.

Preferred sectors are energy and climate, but a strong data science role in another sector is still interesting.

## What to exclude

Work that is mainly administrative, sales or marketing, customer support, manual labelling for AI, pure web or mobile development, pure project coordination, manufacturing, installation or maintenance, and experimental lab research.

Reporting limited to accounting, budgets or sales KPIs is not enough; it becomes interesting only when the role builds models or reusable data tools.

## Constraints

- At most two years of mandatory experience; preferred experience does not exclude.
- Languages: English and German. Other languages exclude only when they are mandatory.
- No internships, theses or student-only positions. A funded PhD position is still possible.
- No limits on country, salary, remote policy or tech stack.

When a role is mixed, or only the company description is available, mark it as “to verify” rather than excluding it.
